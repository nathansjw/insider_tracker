    How it works

    From raw SEC filings to a flagged cluster, every weekday
    1

    Collect

    A scheduled GitHub Actions job pulls every Form 4 listed in SEC EDGAR's daily filing index.

    2

    Filter

    Keeps only open-market purchases (transaction code P) worth $50K+. Sales, option exercises, grants, gifts, and funds that are only 10% owners are dropped.

    3

    Detect

    Groups purchases by company and flags a cluster when enough distinct insiders buy inside the window. All filters on this page re-run detection live.

    4

    Alert

    Pushes a digest to subscribers' phones and republishes this dashboard with the new data.

    Read this before acting on a cluster

    • Why clusters matter: academic research links clustered insider buying to later outperformance. The effect is real but modest, and it varies from year to year.
    • Timing: clusters right after an earnings release can just mean the trading window opened. Mid-quarter clusters tend to say more.
    • Pre-planned trades: 10b5-1 plan purchases are scheduled in advance and say little about current conviction. Check the filing footnotes on EDGAR.
    • Use: this is a research-prioritization tool that tells you where to look. It is not a trading signal or investment advice.
