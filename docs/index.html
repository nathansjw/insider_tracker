Alerts & method

Get real-time alerts

Each weekday after the pipeline runs, a digest of flagged clusters is pushed to an ntfy topic. No account needed:

  1. Install the ntfy app (iOS / Android), or open it in your browser.
  2. Subscribe to topic insider-cluster-30f8c67d9a94.

This page refreshes its data on the same schedule. Hit reload after ~7:30am ET.

How it works

  1. Pulls every Form 4 from SEC EDGAR's daily index.
  2. Keeps only open-market purchases (code P) of $50K+, which drops sales, option exercises, grants and gifts. Funds that are only 10% owners are excluded.
  3. Groups purchases by company and flags a cluster when enough distinct insiders buy within the window.

Clustered insider buying has a documented association with later outperformance, but this is a research-prioritization tool, not a trading signal. Clusters right after earnings can just mean the trading window opened.

Data: SEC EDGAR Form 4 filings, updated each weekday by a GitHub Actions job. Not investment advice.
